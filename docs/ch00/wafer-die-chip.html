Wafer, die and chip

A wafer carries many identical circuits, each called a die. The wafer is cut along the scribe lines and each die is packaged into the chip you can hold.

Follow one die

How many dies fit on a wafer?

Die area S Formula estimate Dies drawn (full dies only) Wafer area used by full dies
DPW ≈ π(d/2)² / S − πd / √(2S)
d = 300 mm (wafer diameter), S = die area in mm²

First term: wafer area divided by die area. Second term: dies lost at the round edge. Ignores scribe lines and edge exclusion; the drawn count is the best of 4 grid alignments. All numbers are illustrative geometry, not data for a real product.
