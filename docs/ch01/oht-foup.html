Dispatch a FOUP with the OHT

Pick a tool and send a FOUP from the stocker. An overhead vehicle carries it on the ceiling rail and lowers it onto the tool's load port.

Tools (click a card's button)

Choose a tool, then press "Dispatch FOUP".

Trip stages

  1. 1. Hoist grabs a FOUP at the stocker
  2. 2. Vehicle rides the rail to the tool
  3. 3. Hoist lowers the FOUP onto the load port
  4. 4. FOUP docked: the tool takes over
