Czochralski crystal growth

Melt polysilicon, touch it with a seed, then turn and pull the seed up very slowly. The silicon that freezes under the seed copies its crystal.

Time-lapse: a real 300 mm ingot takes about a day or more to grow. Scene is a schematic, not to scale.
