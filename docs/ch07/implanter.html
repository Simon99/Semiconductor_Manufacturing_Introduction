Impurity atoms are turned into ions, sorted by mass, accelerated and swept across the wafer. Change the accelerating energy and watch the implant depth move.

Illustrative model, not a process simulator: Rp ≈ 3 · E0.95 nm (E in keV), ΔRp = 0.35 · Rp, Gaussian profile N(x) = Q / (√(2π) ΔRp) · exp(−(x−Rp)² / 2ΔRp²), with dose Q. Real depth depends on ion species, substrate and tilt (channeling). Annealing is not modeled. Dose sets how many atoms; energy sets how deep.
