CMP tool: how pressure and speed set the removal rate

Preston relation
RR = Kp · P · V
V = 2π · r · N / 60 with r = 0.15 m; Kp = 1.0×10−13 Pa−1 (both illustrative)
Removal rate vs pressure (at the current platen speed)

V is the relative speed between wafer and pad at a point 0.15 m from the platen center, with the head spinning at the same rpm as the platen. Kp depends on the pad, slurry and film. Simplified model: Preston's relation says removal rate grows in proportion to pressure and relative speed. It leaves out slurry chemistry, pad wear and pattern density, which matter as much in practice. All numbers on this page are illustrative.
