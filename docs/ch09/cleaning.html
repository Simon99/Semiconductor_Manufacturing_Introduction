Batch wet bench vs single-wafer spin clean

Toggle the mode, then change the sliders. You trade throughput against cross-contamination. All numbers are illustrative.

Stage:

Illustrative model, not measured data: batch cycle 15 min per batch (throughput = batch size x 4 per hour); single-wafer 4 min per wafer per chamber (15 per hour per chamber). Batch contamination index rises as the shared bath ages: 8 + 87 x (1 - e^(-N/80)); single-wafer stays low (5) because every wafer gets fresh chemical. A dirty wafer in a batch shares a bath with the whole lot; in a single-wafer chamber its particles are flung to the drain.
