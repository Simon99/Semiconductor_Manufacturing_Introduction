Watch the prober step the wafer under the probe card, then change the model: how many dies are bad, and how good the needle contact is.

What is happening

  1. 1Prober steps the wafer so the next die sits under the needles.
  2. 2The chuck lifts the wafer and the needles touch the pads.
  3. 3The tester sends signals and checks the reply.
  4. 4The die gets a bin and joins the wafer map.

Real idea: each die is tested in turn and its bin is added to the wafer map. The defect pattern, speed and contact model here are illustrative; in this page Bin 3 means a good die wrongly failed by poor contact.
