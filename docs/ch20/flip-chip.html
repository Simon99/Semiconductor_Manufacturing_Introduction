What changes with the contacts?

Area array, whole die face (N×N)
Edge ring only (4N−4)

Same N×N grid of positions: wire bonding uses the pads along the edge (dark dots); flip chip can use every position across the die face (all dots).

Why underfill?

Largest bump shear (exaggerated)

Bumps are not solder balls: bumps join die to substrate; solder balls join the package to the circuit board.
