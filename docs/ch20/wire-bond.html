Wire bonding: connecting the die to the package

Schematic, not to scale. Press play, jump between steps, or change the loop height and mold flow below.

  • Bond wire: gold or copper, thinner than a human hair
  • Bond pad: small metal square on the die
  • Mold compound: epoxy resin body
  • Die attach adhesive: glues the die to the paddle
  • Die paddle and leads: the lead frame

Wire sweep: of the gap to the neighbor wire
Top view of three neighboring wires (illustrative). Mold flow pushes the middle wire sideways; a taller loop is a longer wire and sweeps more.

Sweep model (illustrative, not measured): sweep% = flow × (wire length / default length)² × 0.6. At 100% the wire touches its neighbor, a short circuit.
