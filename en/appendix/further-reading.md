# Appendix C: Further Reading

This appendix collects the references cited in each chapter, along with books, standards and conferences to explore if you want to go deeper. For terminology, see [Appendix A: Glossary](glossary.md).

---

## Introductory and general

| Resource | Notes | Related chapters |
|---|---|---|
| Peter Van Zant, *Microchip Fabrication: A Practical Guide to Semiconductor Processing* | A classic introduction covering the whole manufacturing flow | Whole book |
| Hong Xiao, *Introduction to Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology* | Organized by process module, with clear illustrations | Part 2 |
| Michael Quirk and Julian Serda, *Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology* | Practical and equipment-focused, good for readers joining the industry | Whole book |
| James D. Plummer, Michael D. Deal and Peter B. Griffin, *Silicon VLSI Technology: Fundamentals, Practice, and Modeling* | More theoretical; Chapter 2 walks through a complete CMOS flow | Parts 2 and 3 |
